In Angular, Forms are of two types:

* Template-driven Forms
* Model-driven Forms, also known as **Reactive Forms**

Reactive Forms are used when there is some immutable data model, which is mapped to a database.  
To learn more about the working of Reactive Forms, refer to the blog post: [**Reactive (Model-driven) Forms in Angular**](https://nishugoel.wordpress.com/2018/10/17/angular-model-driven-reactive-forms/) **or**
